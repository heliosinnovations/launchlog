Features

Everything you need to showcase your work

Built for developers who want their portfolio to reflect their actual work, not their ability to maintain a portfolio.

Auto-sync from GitHub

README changes, new commits, stars, releases - all reflected instantly on your showcase page.

Embeddable widgets

Drop a script tag in your portfolio. It stays fresh forever and matches your site's styling.

Smart screenshots

Auto-captures project screenshots from live URLs or repo images. No manual uploads needed.

Social proof

Shows HackerNews discussions, Reddit threads, and other mentions. Like citations for your code.

Zero maintenance

Build. Push. Showcase. That's it. Your portfolio updates automatically when you ship.

Dark mode ready

Widgets inherit your site's CSS variables. Looks native on any theme, light or dark.

Widget Styles

Three ways to showcase

Choose the style that fits your site. All widgets inherit your design system.
